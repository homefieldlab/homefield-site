How we work

Built with you. Yours to own.

Every engagement follows the same path. Here is exactly what working with Homefield looks like, start to finish.

Social media was the last inflection point in sports. AI is the next one.

AI has collapsed the cost of building real software, and the organizations that move now will build advantages that compound and are hard to close. We're a product-led dev shop: we help sports organizations, and the startups building in the space, make that move, not by selling you another tool, but by building custom technology with you, for you, until it is yours. Here is how that works.

01

Start with a Social Tech Audit

Every engagement starts here. For a one-time fee, credited toward whatever we build, we take a clear-eyed look at your technology today, social and beyond: where it works, where it quietly costs you, and where the real opportunity is. You walk away with a concrete plan whether or not we build it together. It is the lowest-friction way to begin, and it turns the conversation from a sales pitch into a diagnosis.

02

We build your data foundation

Everything starts with your data. We bring your roster, your schedule, and your social accounts into one place you can finally get the specific answers you want, the way you want them. Roster moves stay current. Your team accounts and your athletes' associated accounts are mapped. This foundation is what makes everything we build possible, and it is the thing off-the-shelf tools never give you.

03

We build in credits

From there, we build. Off-the-shelf modules and bespoke work are priced in credits, a simple unit of build effort, so you always know what you are getting. Everything is AI-accelerated and human-graded: we move fast where AI creates real advantage, then a senior developer reviews and hardens it before it ships. The speed of AI, without the slop.

04

Documented in real time

As we build, we document. Living documentation stays current with the work, so you always know exactly what you have and how it runs. This is the difference between renting a black box and owning a system, you are never dependent on us to understand your own technology.

05

Your data, handled

We treat your data the way you would want it treated. We collect only what the work requires, keep your data yours, and never sell or share it. Access stays limited to the people building your systems. When an engagement ends, you keep your data and your build, nothing is held hostage.

06

Yours to own

We build it, document it, and hand it over on your timeline. The goal is not to lock you in. It is to leave you more capable than we found you. Managed, hybrid, or fully owned, you decide the pace and you end up holding the keys.
